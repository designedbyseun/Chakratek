Exodus Music Group was built as a modern record label platform designed to give independent artists the power, ownership, and infrastructure traditionally reserved for major labels. Chakratek led the project from research to final delivery, crafting both a high-impact public-facing website and a robust admin system to manage artists, releases, and services.

Our work focused on creating a minimal, culture-forward digital presence that reflects Exodus’ mission, putting artists in control of their careers while maintaining a premium, industry-ready standard. The final solution combines bold editorial design, intuitive user flows, and a scalable backend that supports music distribution, publishing, production, and artist management.
