TurnUpLagos was built to define a growing platform in the events space centred around Lagos — live music, and event management tools for organisers. The platform serves as a content-led urban Lagos hub connecting certified vendors, platforms, event information easily promote and manage their own listings.

At Chakratek, we handled the full product structure, from research and experience design to development and scalability planning. We focused on creating a platform that works smoothly for both users searching for interesting events and organisers looking for visibility and control.

The final product features a clean, distinctive interface that users see as a powerful event system that simplifies every element — posting, pricing, and notifications. The platform was designed to scale beyond Lagos, supporting growth into new cities and markets.
