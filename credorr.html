Credorr is a peer-to-peer betting application designed to make football predictions more social, transparent, and engaging. Unlike traditional betting platforms, Credorr allows users to challenge real people on real matches, creating a competitive experience built around trust and community.

Our role spanned the full product lifecycle, from product strategy and UX/UI design to development and implementation. We designed the user flows, interface, and interaction patterns, and also built the platform to ensure performance, reliability, and scalability. The experience was crafted with a strong focus on clarity, speed, and confidence, enabling users to place challenges, track bets, and manage their wallets seamlessly.

A bold yet minimal visual system supports intuitive navigation while reinforcing a sports-driven identity. In addition, we established design and product guidelines to maintain consistency as Credorr evolves with new features and platforms.
